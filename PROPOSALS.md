# Governance Proposals — History

This file records every proposal that changed the governance framework — Constitutional and Governance Process proposals that ratified, activated, amended, or retired a document in this repository. Each entry records where the proposal is authoritatively recorded and which documents it affected.

Treasury, election, temperature check, and executable proposals are recorded on-chain and on the governance forum. They are not logged here unless they also produce a document change in this repository.

Entries are added when a proposal is submitted and updated when a vote concludes.

**Where proposal documents live.** A proposal is drafted in `pending/`, put to the vote, and recorded
on-chain. On passage the proposal document is removed from the repository: the on-chain record is the
authoritative account of what was voted on and how the vote resolved, and the signed, hashed documents
the proposal produced are the authoritative account of what it adopted. A reader who finds no proposal
file in this repository is not looking at a gap in the record — the record is the on-chain entry and
the signed document set identified in each entry below.

## The founding sequence

Under the Operating Agreement, the framework is adopted and activated through a sequence of community acts, not a single founding vote. The Company is **member-managed** at formation and community governance is **advisory** until the Activation Vote:

1. **GP-PRE-1 — Constitutional Ratification** (pre-formation) → ratifies the Charter + policy library (Activation Condition 6)
2. **Formation** → Transition RAC files the Certificate of Formation + Operating Agreement (member-managed)
3. **GP-ELECT-1 — Permanent RAC Election** → Activation Condition 7
4. **GP-ACTIVATE-1 — Activation Vote** → Activation Date; the Company becomes Algorithmically Governed and governance outcomes become binding
