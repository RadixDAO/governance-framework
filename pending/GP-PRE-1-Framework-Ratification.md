# GP-PRE-1: Constitutional Ratification of the Governance Framework

---

> **Governance system note:** This proposal is submitted through the **community's own Consultation V2
> governance system** — the on-chain governance tool the community deployed independently, using
> software donated by the Founding Transferor. The community operates this system in its own right.
> Radix DAO LLC does not yet legally exist as an entity, and this proposal **does not** form it. This
> is the community's **constitutional ratification** of the governance framework, conducted **before
> formation**, so that the Transition RAC carries a community-ratified framework into the formation of
> the Company.
>
> **This is not an activation vote.** Ratifying the framework does not make community governance binding
> and does not activate the DAO. After formation the Company is **member-managed** and community
> governance is **advisory** until the separate, later **Activation Vote** (GP-ACTIVATE-1). See the
> activation sequence below.

## 2. Summary

This proposal is the community's **constitutional ratification** of the complete governance framework of
Radix DAO LLC — the **Charter** (as the DAO's governance constitution) and the **full operational policy
library**, identified by file, version and content hash in §5. It is conducted **before** the legal entity is formed, on the
community's own Consultation V2 system.

Ratification establishes the framework that the Transition RAC carries into formation. It satisfies
**Activation Condition 6** of the Operating Agreement (Schedule 5) and is recognised and given legal
effect by the Operating Agreement upon formation (OA §11.1, §11.2, §11.5; Charter §4A).

It does **not** form the entity (that mandate is already held by the current RAC) and does **not**
activate the DAO. Following formation the Company is **member-managed** and community governance is
**advisory** (OA §§5.7–5.9; Charter §4A) until the binding **Activation Vote** (GP-ACTIVATE-1).

## 4. Rationale

The DAO has developed a comprehensive governance framework over an extended drafting and review period —
the Charter and the operational policy library — designed to pair with the Operating Agreement under an
OA-first legal structure. Under the Operating Agreement, the community's adoption of that framework is a
**constitutional ratification** that must be in place **before** the Permanent RAC election, because the
election runs on the ratified rulebook (the Elections & Role Governance Policy). Ratifying **before
formation** removes that sequencing problem and means the Transition RAC forms the Company already
carrying a community-ratified framework.

This proposal is deliberately **narrow**. The establishment mandate — forming the entity, constituting
the Transition RAC, the Delegate roster, and the Establishment Grant — is **already given** by the
community's formation and approval of the current RAC and is **not** re-sought here. Activation of the
DAO is a **separate, later** act (the Activation Vote). Keeping ratification distinct from activation is
what makes the Activation Vote a clean, informed community sign-off rather than a take-it-or-leave-it
vote on an unseen constitution.

## 6. Effect of this proposal

**On passage:**

1. The Charter and the full policy library are the **community-ratified governance framework** of Radix
   DAO LLC, satisfying **Activation Condition 6(b)** (community constitutional ratification).
2. The Transition RAC adopts the framework by **unanimous written resolution** in connection with
   formation, satisfying **Activation Condition 6(a)**, and publishes the ratified framework to the
   Official Venue (Schedule 5 condition 6, evidence item (c)).
3. The Transition RAC proceeds, under its existing mandate, to form the Company carrying this framework.

**What this proposal does NOT do:**

- It does **not** form the legal entity — the establishment mandate is already held by the current RAC,
  and formation is a Transition RAC formation function (OA §6.5), not a matter re-approved here.
- It does **not** activate the DAO. After formation the Company is **member-managed** (OA §8.1), and
  community governance outcomes are **advisory** (OA §§5.8–5.9; Charter §4A) — save for the three
  binding community acts (this ratification, the Permanent RAC election, and the Activation Vote).
- Binding algorithmic governance begins only on the **Activation Date**, when the **Activation Vote**
  (GP-ACTIVATE-1) passes (OA §§5.7, 8.8).

## 12. Voting Options

- **YES** — Ratify and adopt the Charter and the full policy library as the community's constitutional
  governance framework, to be carried into formation and recognised by the Operating Agreement.
- **NO** — Do not ratify. The framework is not adopted; the standard cooldown applies before
  resubmission.
- **ABSTAIN** — Participate in quorum without expressing a directional preference. ABSTAIN counts toward
  the 10% quorum but is excluded from the ≥66% approval calculation, and does not count toward the
  Minimum Affirmative Support floor (DAO Parameters §3.3A) — ratification requires YES votes of at least
  3.5% of eligible voting power in their own right.
