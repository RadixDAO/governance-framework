# GP-PRE-1 — Constitutional Ratification of the Governance Framework

**Proposal ID** GP-PRE-1 · **Category** Constitutional · **Change Classification** Major
**Author** Transition Radix Accountability Council · **System** Consultation V3, pre-formation
**Thresholds** (DAO Parameters §3A.1): quorum ≥ 10% of eligible voting power; approval ≥ 66% YES of Decisive Votes; Minimum Affirmative Support ≥ 3.5% of eligible voting power in YES (§3.3A).

## 1. Operative clause

The community constitutionally ratifies and adopts the documents listed in §2, **at the versions and content hashes there stated** and signed with the certificate identified in **§2A**, as the governance framework of Radix DAO LLC: the Charter as the DAO's governance constitution, and the remainder as its operational policy library.

This is the community act referred to in Operating Agreement Schedule 5, condition 6(b). On formation, the Company ratifies, adopts and gives legal effect to this vote and to the version of the framework it approves — OA §11.1A (recognition of pre-formation ratification), §11.1 (Charter), §11.2 and §11.5 (subordinate governance rules).

## 3. Effect on passage

1. The documents in §2 are the community-ratified governance framework, satisfying Activation Condition 6(b).
2. The Transition RAC adopts the same framework by unanimous written resolution in connection with formation (condition 6(a)) and publishes it to the Official Venue (condition 6, evidence (c)).
3. The Transition RAC proceeds, under its existing mandate, to form the Company carrying this framework.

## 4. What this proposal does not do

* It does **not** form the legal entity. The establishment mandate is already held by the current RAC and is not re-sought here.
* It does **not** activate the DAO. After formation the Company is member-managed (OA §8.1) and community governance is advisory (OA §§5.8–5.9; Charter §4A) until the Activation Vote (GP-ACTIVATE-1) passes (OA §§5.7, 8.8).
* It confers **no new authority** on the RAC.

## 5. Required proposal content (Proposal & Voting Framework §5)

* **Rationale.** The framework must be ratified before the Permanent RAC election, because that election runs on the ratified Elections & Role Governance Policy. Ratifying before formation removes the sequencing problem and lets the Transition RAC form the Company already carrying a community-ratified framework.
* **Expected outcomes.** Condition 6 satisfied and recorded; the framework published to the Official Venue before formation; GP-ELECT-1 able to proceed.
* **Risks and trade-offs.** Failure to reach 66% / 10% — mitigated by the Temperature Check period. Conflation of ratification with activation — addressed in §4. Late changes to the framework after ratification — handled under the Governance Maintenance & Upgrade Framework and, for entrenched provisions, OA §12.2.
* **Conflict of interest.** Inherent founding conflict: the Transition RAC authored the framework it asks the community to ratify. Mitigated by the public Temperature Check, the community's ability to reject, and §4 (no new authority conferred).
* **Budget / execution plan.** None. This proposal moves no funds and requires no on-chain execution.

## 6. Voting options

* **YES** — Ratify and adopt the documents in §2 as the community's constitutional governance framework.
* **NO** — Do not ratify. The framework is not adopted; the standard cooldown applies before resubmission.
* **ABSTAIN** — Counts toward the 10% quorum; excluded from the 66% approval calculation; does not count toward the Minimum Affirmative Support floor.
