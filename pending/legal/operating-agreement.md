## NOTICE OF RESTRICTIONS ON DUTIES AND TRANSFERS

The rights of members in a decentralised autonomous organisation may differ materially from the rights of members in other limited liability companies. The Marshall Islands DAO Act, the Certificate of Formation, this Operating Agreement, the Charter, and any relevant governance contracts or smart contracts may define, reduce or eliminate duties, may govern voting and governance rights, and may restrict transfers, withdrawal, resignation, return of contributions, access to information and dissolution.

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Radix community approved the creation of a Marshall Islands DAO LLC as the legal entity to support the governance and development of the Radix decentralised protocol and its associated ecosystem;

WHEREAS, the community approved the current three-member RAC to carry forward the transition to that legal DAO LLC during the Transition Period;

WHEREAS, the Radix Foundation has required that the recipient entity have constitutional documents with aligned objectives, a non-distribution principle, an asset lock or winding-up provision, and a clearly documented governance structure, together with a continuity statement and appropriate compliance arrangements;

WHEREAS, the parties intend that the current three-member RAC shall operate during the Transition Period as the Transition RAC, being a temporary implementation authority responsible for formation, compliance, treasury setup, asset receipt, continuity arrangements and handover;

WHEREAS, the parties further intend that, after the Transition Period and satisfaction of the Activation Conditions, the Company shall be algorithmically governed through the governance mechanism recognised by this Agreement and the Charter, with the Permanent RAC acting as guardian of the governance process and constitutional order rather than as a manager;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows.

## ARTICLE I — DEFINITIONS

**1.1 "Activation Conditions"** means the conditions specified in this Agreement and the Charter which must be satisfied before the Company becomes Algorithmically Governed, as set out in binary and evidentially defined form in Schedule 5.

**1.2 "Activation Date"** means the date on which the Activation Vote passes in accordance with the requirements of this Agreement and the Charter, being the date on which the Transition Period ends, and the Company becomes Algorithmically Governed.

**1.3 "Activation Statement"** means the statement published by the Transition RAC to the Official Venue confirming satisfaction of each Activation Condition set out in Schedule 5, together with the corresponding evidential proofs, in accordance with clause 8.5 of this Agreement.

**1.4 "Activation Vote"** means the vote conducted through the Governance Mechanism by Governance Participants confirming that each Activation Condition set out in Schedule 5 has been satisfied, the mechanics of which are set out in the Charter, and which constitutes the sole binding governance vote of the Company during the Advisory Governance Period.

**1.5 "Advisory Governance Period"** means the period commencing on the date of formation of the Company and ending on the Activation Date, during which governance outcomes produced through the Governance Mechanism are advisory only, save for the Activation Vote.

**1.6 "Algorithmically Governed"** means that, following the Transition Period and satisfaction of the Activation Conditions, the governance of the Company is carried out through the Governance Mechanism recognised by this Agreement and the Charter, including the relevant governance contracts, smart contracts, governance platform, voting rules, thresholds, and related constitutional procedures, such that governance outcomes determined in accordance with those rules become binding decisions of the Company, while off-chain implementation, legal execution, compliance, custody, and other non-automated functions may continue to be performed by authorised Persons or bodies where required by this Agreement, the Charter, or applicable law.

**1.7 "Asset Transferor"** means any Person or entity transferring or granting assets, rights, funds, credentials, or control positions to the Company.

**1.8 "Charter"** means the constitutional governance document of the Company, as adopted and amended in accordance with this Agreement, setting out the more detailed governance framework of the Company.

**1.8A "On-Chain Identifiers & Verification Policy"** means the operational policy of that name forming part of the DAO's governance framework and recognised under the Charter, which carries the authoritative register of the Company's governance and treasury on-chain identifiers, the rules for their configuration and use, and the means of their public verification, as in force from time to time in accordance with clause 11.4.

**1.9 "Company"** means Radix DAO LLC, a Marshall Islands non-profit DAO LLC formed pursuant to the Marshall Islands DAO Act 2022 and the applicable laws of the Republic of the Marshall Islands.

**1.10 "Founding Transferor"** means the principal transferor of assets, rights, funds, credentials, and control positions to the Company at or around the time of formation, the identity of whom is recorded in the Continuity Statement executed pursuant to Article IX of this Agreement and is not required to be named in this Agreement. References in this Agreement to the Founding Transferor shall be construed as references to the person whose identity is so recorded and shall remain valid notwithstanding any subsequent change of name, corporate restructuring, dissolution or replacement of that person, provided always that the Continuity Statement is updated accordingly.

**1.11 "Governance Mechanism"** means the on-chain or hybrid governance system recognised by this Agreement and the Charter through which Governance Participants submit proposals, vote, and determine governance outcomes binding on the Company in accordance with the applicable rules, thresholds, and procedures. For the purposes of Schedule 5 condition 6(b) only, "Governance Mechanism" also includes the community's pre-existing governance system in use for the constitutional ratification vote conducted before the Company's formation, the record of which is recognised and adopted by the Company under clause 11.1A.

**1.12 "Governance Participant"** means a Person, other than the Company in respect of Governance Tokens beneficially owned by it, who holds Governance Tokens and participates in the governance system of the Company in accordance with this Agreement, the Charter, and any subordinate governance rules validly adopted thereunder. Governance Participation does not, of itself, confer Membership.

**1.13 "Governance Participation"** means participation in the governance system of the Company by a Governance Participant through the Governance Mechanism recognised by this Agreement and the Charter.

**1.14 "Governance Token"** means any token or token class recognised by this Agreement, the Charter, or subordinate governance rules as conferring eligibility to participate in the governance system of the Company.

**1.15 "Legal Signatory"** means the Person authorised in accordance with this Agreement, the Charter, or a valid governance decision to execute contracts, continuity statements, transfer documents, custody documents, regulatory filings, and other legal instruments on behalf of the Company.

**1.16 "Member"** means a Person who holds Membership in the Company in accordance with this Agreement. For the avoidance of doubt, holding a Governance Token, participating in governance, contributing to the protocol, or otherwise participating in the wider Radix ecosystem shall not, of itself, constitute Membership in the Company.

**1.17 "Membership"** means the legal status of being a Member of the Company under this Agreement. During the Transition Period, Membership shall be held by the members of the Transition RAC. Following the Transition Period, Membership shall be held by the members of the Permanent RAC, unless and to the extent otherwise provided by this Agreement, the Charter, or a valid governance decision adopted in accordance with them. The broader class of Governance Participants or token-holders need not be identical to the legal Membership of the Company at all times.

**1.18 "Official Venue"** means the official online venue or venues of the Company identified in Schedule 3 and publicly designated in accordance with this Agreement and the Charter, being the venue or venues at which governance records, activation statements, status reports, advisory acknowledgements, and other official communications of the Company are published.

**1.19 "Permanent RAC"** means the Radix Accountability Council elected by the community during the Transition Period, after satisfaction of Activation Condition 6, in accordance with the Charter and the Elections Policy forming part thereof, and formally seated prior to the Activation Vote as required by Activation Condition 7, which assumes its constitutional role as the standing governance-process oversight body of the Company with effect from the Activation Date. The Permanent RAC is the guardian of the governance process and constitutional order of the Company and is not, unless expressly stated otherwise, a manager, general executive, treasury-management body, or policy-making authority.

**1.20 "Person"** means an individual, company, corporation, partnership, or any body incorporated or unincorporated, and includes every director, manager, agent, or secretary of such Person.

**1.21 "Transition Period"** means the period commencing on the date the Company is formed and ending on the Activation Date.

**1.22 "Transition RAC"** means the temporary Transition Radix Accountability Council constituted under this Agreement for the limited purpose of implementing the Transition Period, including completion of the formation of the Company, establishment of the transition treasury and related control architecture, satisfaction of initial compliance and onboarding requirements, receipt and safeguarding of assets, rights and control positions transferred to the Company, execution of continuity arrangements, and implementation of the handover to the Permanent RAC and the long-term governance framework. The Transition RAC is a temporary implementation authority only and shall have no continuing or general governance authority except as expressly set out in this Agreement and the Charter.

**1.23 "Covered Person"** means any of the following persons, in the exercise of their role in relation to the Company: (i) a Member (whether a Transition RAC member or a Permanent RAC member); (ii) a Governance Participant; (iii) a Transition RAC member or Permanent RAC member; (iv) a Legal Signatory or signer; and (v) any other person acting in an authorised role under this Agreement, the Charter, or a valid governance decision.

**1.24 "Seated"** means, in relation to a member of the Transition RAC or the Permanent RAC, a person who: (a) has been duly appointed or elected to their position on the relevant body; (b) has accepted this Agreement and, where applicable, the Charter, in accordance with clause 4.5; (c) has satisfied all applicable onboarding, compliance, KYC, and eligibility requirements for that role; and (d) has not ceased to hold that position by reason of resignation, removal, disqualification, or cessation of Membership in accordance with clauses 4.7 and 4.8. A person who has formally withdrawn from or resigned their position shall not be treated as Seated.

## ARTICLE II — ORGANISATION AND FORMATION

**2.1 Formation.** The Members, by executing this Agreement, hereby agree to organise as a Marshall Islands non-profit member-managed decentralised autonomous organisation limited liability company during the Transition Period pursuant to the laws of the Republic of the Marshall Islands, including the DAO Act 2022, the Limited Liability Company Act 1996, the Non-Profit Entities Act 2020, the DAO Regulations 2024, and related applicable law.

**2.2 Name.** The name of the Company shall be Radix DAO LLC.

**2.3 DAO Status.** The Company is a decentralised autonomous organisation for the purposes of the Marshall Islands DAO Act 2022. The conspicuous notice of restrictions on duties and transfers appears at the beginning of this Agreement.

**2.4 Registered Agent and Registered Office.** The registered office of the Company in the Marshall Islands shall be the initial registered office designated in the Certificate of Formation or such other office as provided by law. The registered agent of the Company in the Marshall Islands shall be the initial registered agent designated in the Certificate of Formation.

**2.5 Official Online Venue.** The official online venue or venues of the Company shall be those identified in Schedule 3 and publicly designated in accordance with this Agreement and the Charter.

**2.6 Representative Agent.** The Company shall designate a representative agent or point of contact to receive correspondence and communicate with the registered agent and Registrar, in accordance with applicable law.

**2.7 Term.** The Company shall commence upon the date of filing of the Certificate of Formation and shall continue in existence until dissolved pursuant to this Agreement or by operation of law.

## ARTICLE III — OBJECTS, NON-PROFIT CHARACTER AND APPLICATION OF PROPERTY

**3.1 Non-Profit Character.** The Company shall be a non-profit entity within the meaning of the Non-Profit Entities Act 2020 and may exercise the powers and privileges conferred upon limited liability companies by the laws of the Marshall Islands only in furtherance of and subject to its company purpose.

**3.2 Objects.** The Company is established exclusively for the following objects:

(a) education, research, development and management of decentralised ledgers, exchanges, cryptographic systems and technologies;

(b) support for knowledge creation, exchange and research in associated technologies;

(c) promotion of knowledge and education in, and the use, operation and governance of, decentralised networks, blockchains and complementary or alternative technologies; and

(d) creation, development and provision of governance for distributed ledger and decentralised protocols and the distribution and exchange of cryptographic tokens.

**3.3 Income and Property.** The income and property of the Company, from wherever derived, shall be applied solely in promoting the objects of the Company.

**3.4 Non-Distribution Principle.** No distribution shall be paid, and no capital or property of the Company shall be returned, transferred or distributed to any Member in cash or otherwise, except as expressly permitted by this Agreement.

**3.5 Permitted Payments.** Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Company from making, in good faith:

(a) reasonable and proper remuneration to any Member, officer, contractor, employee or other person for any services rendered to the Company;

(b) payment of interest on money lent to the Company by any Member or other person at a reasonable and proper rate;

(c) payment of reasonable and proper rent for premises leased or let to the Company; or

(d) reimbursement of reasonable out-of-pocket expenses properly incurred on behalf of the Company.

**3.6 Authority of the Company.** The Company shall have and exercise all powers conferred by Marshall Islands law on a non-profit DAO LLC and such further powers as are necessary or incidental to carrying out its objects, subject always to this Agreement, the Charter, and applicable law.

**3.7 Further Non-Profit Limitation.** The Company shall be operated solely in furtherance of its objects and not for the primary purpose of carrying on a trade or business for profit.

## ARTICLE IV — MEMBERS AND MEMBERSHIP

**4.1 Membership of the Company.** The Company shall have Members. Membership in the Company is a legal and constitutional status governed exclusively by this Agreement and, where applicable, the Charter. For the avoidance of doubt, Membership is distinct from Governance Participation, and a Person may be a Governance Participant without being a Member.

**4.2 Members during the Transition Period.** During the Transition Period, the Members of the Company shall be the members of the Transition RAC. A Person shall become a Member during the Transition Period upon:

(a) assuming a seat on the Transition RAC in accordance with this Agreement and the Charter;

(b) accepting this Agreement and, where applicable, the Charter, in accordance with clause 4.5; and

(c) satisfying any applicable onboarding, compliance, KYC, eligibility, or other requirements applicable to that role or Membership status.

**4.3 Members following the Transition Period.** Following the Transition Period, the Members of the Company shall be the members of the Permanent RAC, unless and to the extent otherwise provided by this Agreement, the Charter, or a valid governance decision adopted in accordance with them. A Person shall become a Member following the Transition Period upon:

(a) assuming a seat on the Permanent RAC, or such other office, seat, or role to which Membership is attached under this Agreement, the Charter, or a valid governance decision adopted in accordance with them;

(b) accepting this Agreement and, where applicable, the Charter, in accordance with clause 4.5; and

(c) satisfying any applicable onboarding, compliance, KYC, eligibility, or other requirements applicable to that office, seat, role, or Membership status.

**4.4 No automatic Membership from Governance Participation.** Holding a Governance Token, participating in governance, contributing to the protocol, or otherwise participating in the wider Radix ecosystem shall not, of itself, confer Membership in the Company. Governance Participation does not, of itself, constitute admission to Membership.

**4.5 Method of Acceptance.** Acceptance of this Agreement and, where applicable, the Charter, may be effected by any one or more of the following methods, as prescribed or approved by the Company:

(a) written signature;

(b) cryptographic or wallet-based execution or attestation;

(c) execution of a joinder, acceptance instrument, or equivalent record; or

(d) such other reliable method of acceptance as may be prescribed by the Company in accordance with this Agreement and the Charter.

**4.6 Commencement of Membership.** Membership shall commence when the relevant Person has satisfied all of the following:

(a) the Person has assumed the office, seat, or role to which Membership is attached under this Agreement or the Charter;

(b) the Person has accepted this Agreement and, where applicable, the Charter, in accordance with clause 4.5; and

(c) the Person has satisfied any applicable onboarding, compliance, KYC, eligibility, or other requirements applicable to that office, seat, role, or Membership status.

**4.7 Cessation of Membership.** A Person shall cease to be a Member immediately upon the earliest of:

(a) ceasing to hold the office, seat, or role by virtue of which Membership was conferred;

(b) resignation from that office, seat, or role, where resignation is permitted;

(c) removal from that office, seat, or role in accordance with this Agreement, the Charter, or the applicable governance process;

(d) failure, suspension, or disqualification in relation to any compliance, KYC, eligibility, or other requirement applicable to Membership, where this Agreement, the Charter, or applicable law so requires; or

(e) dissolution of the Company.

**4.8 Effect of Cessation.** Upon cessation of Membership, the relevant Person shall cease to have any rights, powers, or standing that arise solely by virtue of Membership, save to the extent that any surviving obligations, protections, indemnities, confidentiality obligations, or continuing provisions of this Agreement, the Charter, or applicable law remain in force. For the avoidance of doubt, cessation of Membership does not, of itself, affect any Governance Participation rights which that Person may separately hold by virtue of Governance Tokens, unless otherwise provided by this Agreement, the Charter, or applicable law.

**4.9 No Economic Rights.** Neither Membership nor Governance Participation confers any economic ownership in the Company's assets or any right to share in the income, profits, or distributions of the Company, subject only to the permitted payments expressly provided for in this Agreement.

**4.10 Legal Membership and Governance Participation Distinguished.** The broader class of Governance Participants or token-holders need not be identical to the legal Membership of the Company at all times. Accordingly, the fact that the Company is or becomes Algorithmically Governed does not require every Governance Participant or token-holder to be admitted as a Member of the Company.

## ARTICLE V — GOVERNANCE PARTICIPATION AND GOVERNANCE EFFECT

**5.1 Governance Participation.** The Company shall recognise Governance Participation in accordance with this Agreement, the Charter, and the Governance Mechanism. A Person may be a Governance Participant without being a Member.

**5.2 Governance Participants.** A Governance Participant is a Person who holds Governance Tokens and participates in the governance system of the Company in accordance with this Agreement, the Charter, and any subordinate governance rules validly adopted thereunder. Governance Participation does not, of itself, confer Membership in the Company.

**5.3 Governance Tokens.** A Person shall be eligible to participate as a Governance Participant only to the extent that such Person holds Governance Tokens recognised for governance purposes under this Agreement, the Charter, or the subordinate governance rules validly adopted thereunder.

**5.4 Exercise of Governance Rights.** Governance Participants shall exercise governance rights through the Governance Mechanism recognised by this Agreement and the Charter. Such governance rights may include, to the extent provided for in this Agreement, the Charter, or subordinate governance rules validly adopted thereunder:

(a) proposing or supporting proposals;

(b) voting on proposals, governance changes, or other governance matters;

(c) participating in consultation, review, or other governance processes; and

(d) such other governance functions as may be recognised by the Company's governance framework.

**5.5 Governance Participation Distinguished from Membership.** Governance Participation is distinct from Membership. Accordingly:

(a) a Governance Participant need not be a Member;

(b) a Member need not hold Governance Tokens except to the extent required by this Agreement or the Charter; and

(c) the broader class of Governance Participants or token-holders need not be identical to the legal Membership of the Company at all times.

**5.6 Governance Mechanism.** The Governance Mechanism may include governance contracts, smart contracts, voting interfaces, recognised governance platforms, and such related procedures as are identified in this Agreement, the Charter, or subordinate governance rules validly adopted thereunder.

**5.7 Binding Effect of Governance Outcomes after Activation.** Following the Activation Date, governance outcomes validly determined through the Governance Mechanism in accordance with this Agreement and the Charter shall constitute binding decisions of the Company, subject always to:

(a) this Agreement;

(b) the Charter;

(c) applicable law; and

(d) any procedural or constitutional limits validly imposed under them.

**5.8 Governance during the Advisory Governance Period.** The period from the date of formation to the Activation Date shall constitute the Advisory Governance Period. During the Advisory Governance Period:

(a) Governance Participation may operate through the Governance Mechanism in accordance with this Agreement and the Charter;

(b) governance outcomes adopted through the Governance Mechanism shall constitute advisory expressions of community sentiment only and shall not constitute binding decisions of the Company, save that the Activation Vote shall, upon satisfaction of the requirements set out in this Agreement and the Charter, constitute the sole binding governance decision of the Company during the Advisory Governance Period;

(c) the Company shall remain subject to the limited implementation authority of the Transition RAC and shall not be treated as fully Algorithmically Governed until the Activation Date; and

(d) for the avoidance of doubt, no governance outcome adopted during the Advisory Governance Period, other than the Activation Vote, shall bind the Company beyond the scope permitted by this Agreement, the Charter, and the transitional governance framework.

**5.9 Transition RAC duty to have regard to advisory outcomes.** The Transition RAC shall have regard to advisory governance outcomes in the exercise of its mandate. Where an advisory governance outcome achieves the participation and majority thresholds for recognition specified in the Charter, the Transition RAC shall, within the period specified in the Charter following publication of that outcome, publish a written acknowledgement of the outcome to the Official Venue and, where it determines not to act in accordance with it, a written explanation of the basis for that determination. Such acknowledgements and explanations shall form part of the governance record of the Company. The foregoing does not limit the authority of the Transition RAC to act within its mandate in accordance with this Agreement and the Charter.

**5.10 No Economic Rights from Governance Participation.** Governance Participation does not confer any economic ownership in the Company's assets or any right to share in the income, profits, or distributions of the Company, subject only to the permitted payments expressly provided for in this Agreement.

**5.11 Good Faith in Governance Participation.** Governance Participants shall exercise their governance rights in good faith and in accordance with this Agreement, the Charter, and any applicable governance rules.

**5.12 Recognition of Governance Records.** The Company may recognise the records of the Governance Mechanism, including proposal records, vote records, and governance determinations, as authoritative evidence of governance participation and governance outcomes, subject to this Agreement, the Charter, and applicable law.

**5.13 Authoritative Governance Record.** The Governance Platform or other recognised Governance Mechanism identified in this Agreement, the Charter, or the subordinate governance rules shall constitute the authoritative record of Governance Proposals, votes, proposal outcomes, and governance determinations of the Company, subject to this Agreement, the Charter, and applicable law.

## ARTICLE VI — TRANSITION PERIOD AND TRANSITION RAC

**6.1 Recognition of the Transition Period.** The Company recognises a Transition Period commencing on the date of formation and ending on the Activation Date.

**6.2 Constitution of the Transition RAC.** The current three-member RAC shall act during the Transition Period as the Transition RAC.

**6.3 Composition.** The Transition RAC shall consist of the three members approved by the community, as identified in Schedule 1.

**6.4 Acceptance and Mandate.** Each of the three members of the Transition RAC identified in Schedule 1 has accepted this Agreement in accordance with clause 4.5 and satisfied the applicable onboarding, eligibility, and KYC requirements. The mandate of the Transition RAC commences upon the date of execution of this Agreement and continues until the Activation Date or earlier termination in accordance with this Agreement.

**6.5 Mandate of the Transition RAC.** The mandate of the Transition RAC is strictly limited to the following transition-period functions:

(a) completion of the formation of the Company, including filing and organisational and structural steps;

(b) execution or approval of the constitutional and governance documents required for the Transition Period;

(c) satisfaction of initial KYC, BOIR, onboarding and compliance requirements in accordance with Marshall Islands applicable law;

(d) establishment of the treasury, custody and signer framework, including any initial PrimeVault or equivalent custody arrangements approved for transition purposes;

(e) receipt, safeguarding and documentation of assets, rights, and control positions transferred to the Company, including web2 credentials and related recovery arrangements;

(f) execution of the continuity statement and related transfer documentation;

(g) coordination of the constitution of the Permanent RAC and the post-transition governance framework; and

(h) implementation of the handover to the Permanent RAC and long-term governance framework.

**6.6 Decision Rule of the Transition RAC.** Unless the Charter provides a higher threshold for a specified matter, decisions of the Transition RAC within its mandate shall be made by simple majority of seated Transition RAC members.

**6.7 Limits on Transition RAC Authority.** The Transition RAC is a temporary implementation authority only and has no continuing or general executive, policy-making or treasury-allocation authority except as expressly set out in this Agreement and the Charter.

**6.8 Internal Process and Transparency.** The internal process, transparency obligations, record-keeping obligations, and any additional decision thresholds of the Transition RAC shall be set out in the Charter.

**6.9 Transition Controllers / Founding Transferor-Facing Role.** For Founding Transferor due-diligence and transition purposes, the Transition RAC members may be identified as the natural-person transition controllers of the Company during the Transition Period, without thereby creating any permanent manager status after the Company becomes Algorithmically Governed.

**6.10 Sunset and Handover.** Upon the Activation Date, the mandate of the Transition RAC shall expire automatically save to the extent strictly necessary to complete any residual handover step already approved in accordance with this Agreement and the Charter.

**6.11 Vacancy and Replacement.** If a seat on the Transition RAC becomes vacant before the Activation Date by reason of death, permanent incapacity, resignation, or any other cause, the following procedure shall apply:

(a) the remaining seated Transition RAC members shall, by unanimous written resolution, promptly declare the seat vacant and publish notice of the vacancy to the Official Venue;

(b) a replacement member shall be identified by a recognised community advisory outcome achieving the elevated recognition threshold applicable to Entrenched Provision matters specified in the Charter (being the threshold referred to in clause 12.2(c)(ii));

(c) before being seated, the replacement member shall: (i) satisfy all applicable onboarding, compliance, KYC, and eligibility requirements; (ii) accept this Agreement and, where applicable, the Charter, in accordance with clause 4.5; and (iii) be recorded as a replacement member in Schedule 1 by written resolution of the then-seated Transition RAC members;

(d) Membership and all signer and signatory rights of the replacement member shall commence upon satisfaction of the conditions in clause 6.11(c) and not before; and

(e) where no seated Transition RAC members remain to declare a vacancy, the Registered Agent may publish notice of the vacancy to the Official Venue on behalf of the Company, and the community advisory outcome procedure in clause 6.11(b) shall apply accordingly; and

(f) a seat is deemed vacant for the purposes of this clause 6.11 where the member holding it has been wholly uncontactable or non-participating in the business of the Transition RAC for a continuous period of not less than twenty-one days, following not fewer than three documented attempts at contact through all known communication channels made by the remaining seated Transition RAC members and recorded, together with their dates, methods, and outcomes, to the Official Venue. A declaration of vacancy under this clause 6.11(f) is made by unanimous written resolution of the remaining seated Transition RAC members and does not, of itself, terminate the Membership of the relevant member, but has the effect that the relevant member is not treated as Seated for the purposes of clauses 9.11 and 12.2(c) until the vacancy is filled in accordance with this clause 6.11 or the member resumes active participation and notifies the Company accordingly, whichever first occurs.

## ARTICLE VII — PERMANENT RAC

**7.1 Election and Seating of the Permanent RAC.** The Permanent RAC shall be elected by the community during the Transition Period, after satisfaction of Activation Condition 6 (ratification of the Charter and full policy framework), in accordance with the Charter and the Elections Policy forming part thereof. The Permanent RAC shall be formally seated, with written acceptance from each elected member, prior to the Activation Vote, as required by Activation Condition 7. The Permanent RAC shall assume its constitutional role under this Agreement and the Charter with effect from the Activation Date. For the avoidance of doubt, the election and seating of the Permanent RAC during the Transition Period constitutes a precondition to the Activation Vote and does not, of itself, confer on the Permanent RAC any authority or role prior to the Activation Date.

**7.2 Role of the Permanent RAC.** The Permanent RAC is the guardian of the governance process and constitutional order of the Company and is not, unless expressly stated otherwise, a manager, general executive, treasury-management body, or policy-making authority.

**7.3 No Managerial Status.** The Permanent RAC shall not be treated as the manager of the Company merely by reason of its governance-process oversight role.

**7.4 Detailed Framework in Charter.** The composition, candidacy rules, election mechanics, term, removal process, reporting obligations and detailed mandate of the Permanent RAC shall be governed by the Charter and any subordinate governance rules validly adopted thereunder.

**7.5 No Automatic Continuity from Transition RAC.** Service on the Transition RAC shall not create any automatic continuity into the Permanent RAC.

**7.6 Candidacy and Eligibility.** Former Transition RAC members may stand as candidates for the Permanent RAC on the same basis as other eligible candidates if they satisfy the applicable eligibility requirements set out in the Charter and any subordinate governance rules validly adopted thereunder.

## ARTICLE VIII — ALGORITHMICALLY GOVERNED MODEL, ACTIVATION AND HANDOVER

**8.1 Long-Term Governance Model.** During the Transition Period, the Company shall be member-managed solely for formation and implementation purposes, with the Transition RAC and such authorised persons carrying out the transitional functions set out in this Agreement and the Charter. Following the Transition Period and satisfaction of the Activation Conditions, the Company shall become Algorithmically Governed, and the Certificate of Formation and this Agreement may be amended, where necessary, to reflect algorithmic management under applicable Marshall Islands law.

**8.2 Transition Governance Override.** During the Transition Period, the transitional governance framework in this Agreement and the Charter shall prevail over any assumption that the Company is already fully Algorithmically Governed.

**8.3 Activation Date — Activation Vote.** The Activation Date shall be the date on which the Activation Vote passes in accordance with this Agreement and the Charter. No resolution of the Transition RAC alone shall constitute or substitute for the Activation Vote as the means by which the Activation Date is determined. For the avoidance of doubt, the Transition RAC has no unilateral authority to declare or defer the Activation Date.

**8.4 Activation Conditions.** The Activation Conditions are those set out in Schedule 5 to this Agreement, as supplemented by any additional conditions specified in the Charter. Each condition in Schedule 5 is stated in binary terms and is accompanied by the evidential proof required to establish its satisfaction. The conditions set out in Schedule 5 include, at minimum:

(a) valid formation of the Marshall Islands DAO LLC in accordance with applicable law;

(b) completion of initial compliance, KYC and BOIR processes to the extent required for launch;

(c) establishment of the treasury, custody, and signer framework;

(d) identification of the relevant Governance Mechanism, governance contracts or smart contracts, voting mechanism, and official online venues;

(e) formal ratification by the community of the Charter and full policy framework through a constitutional vote conducted through the Governance Mechanism;

(f) completion of the Permanent RAC election and formal seating of at least the minimum number of Permanent RAC members specified in the Charter, conducted under the community-ratified Charter and Elections Policy;

(g) completion of the principal continuity and asset-transfer steps required for handover; and

(h) completion of the handover conditions specified in this Agreement and the Charter.

**8.5 Activation Statement and Transition RAC Publication Duty.** Upon being satisfied that each condition set out in Schedule 5 has been met, supported by the evidential proof specified therein, the Transition RAC shall promptly publish to the Official Venue an Activation Statement confirming the satisfaction of each condition together with the corresponding evidence. The Transition RAC shall initiate or procure the initiation of the Activation Vote within fourteen days of publication of the Activation Statement. For the avoidance of doubt:

(a) the Transition RAC shall have no discretion to delay publication of the Activation Statement once the evidential proofs for all Schedule 5 conditions are in its possession; and

(b) the Transition RAC shall have no discretion to delay initiation of the Activation Vote once the Activation Statement has been published.

Failure by the Transition RAC to comply with this clause shall constitute a breach of its obligations under this Agreement.

**8.6 Activation Vote.** The Activation Vote is the vote conducted through the Governance Mechanism by which Governance Participants confirm that each Activation Condition set out in Schedule 5 has been satisfied. The Activation Vote is the sole binding governance vote of the Company during the Advisory Governance Period. The mechanics of the Activation Vote, including the voting period, the quorum threshold, the majority required for the vote to carry, and the mechanics applicable in the event the vote fails to reach quorum or majority, shall be set out in the Charter. Subject to the Charter mechanics being satisfied, the passing of the Activation Vote shall constitute a binding decision of the Company and shall determine the Activation Date.

**8.7 Foundation-Dependency and External Delay — Safe Harbour.** Delay in the satisfaction of any Activation Condition that is wholly or partly attributable to:

(a) the timing or progress of the Asset Transferor's transfer process;

(b) any regulatory or compliance requirement imposed by applicable law or by the Asset Transferor as a condition of transfer; or

## ARTICLE X — COMPLIANCE, KYC AND BOIR

**10.1 BOIR Filing.** The Company shall submit a Beneficial Owner Information Report at formation and with each annual report, in accordance with the Marshall Islands DAO Act.

**10.2 Statutory Test.** The relevant statutory test is whether a person holds 25% or more of membership interests or voting rights, and if no person does, then who has actual, effective, or sufficient responsibility or control.

**10.3 Role-Holder and Control Analysis.** The existence of a three-member Transition RAC does not mean automatically that all three must be treated as beneficial owners for BOIR purposes. **10.4 KYC.** The Company shall collect KYC information for those persons who are required to be KYC'd under applicable law, transfer requirements, custody requirements or the Charter, including, if reasonably possible, all three Transition RAC members during the Transition Period where they are acting as formal transition controllers, signers or other control persons.
